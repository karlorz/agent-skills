# Context Map

This repository has more than one domain language. Use the context that matches
the work, and do not mix orchestration terms into the research-skill glossary.

## Contexts

- [Dev-loop](./CONTEXT.md) — orchestration engine, workflow profiles, and interview backends
- [Deep research (dev lane)](./skills/deep-research-dev/CONTEXT.md) — experimental research skill, usage review, and production-promotion language

## Relationships

- **Dev-loop → Deep research (dev lane):** idle and investigate cycles may invoke deep-research-dev as an unattended subroutine. That invocation is usage, not a promotion decision.
- **Deep research (dev lane) → Dev-loop:** daily usage reviews can queue follow-up work items; they do not change workflow-profile policy.
